# MADERAACÚSTICA/wl-j005 — direct ImageGen prompts

Use case: `product-mockup`. Image 1 was always the sole reference: `public/images/PUERTAS/MADERAACÚSTICA/wl-j005/original.webp`.

Shared invariant contract: preserve the complete very tall square-post outer frame and straight top rail, plain vertical-grain leaf, exactly two slim parallel full-height vertical grooves forming a narrow strip near the left, exactly two closely spaced horizontal grooves at handle height extending from the right-hand vertical groove across the broad field to the right edge, exactly one dark slim lever on a round rosette and one separate dark round key cylinder below on the left. Front-facing and complete on pure white. NO_LOGO: no logo, brand, badge, text, symbols, watermark, microtext or pseudotext. No extra panels, grooves, seams, glass, decoration, hardware or scene.

Final composition contract: keep the 429:1093 proportion and make the complete door assembly occupy virtually the entire canvas. The target is a visible 2–8 px-equivalent pure-white safety hairline on each side; up to 12 measured pixels is accepted only when native inspection confirms a visually minimal margin and complete outer silhouette. Broad padding and clipped outer edges are forbidden. Close framing must be produced directly by ImageGen; no crop, resize or bitmap processing is allowed.

Independent finish requests: deep matte black (`negro`), very dark espresso wenge (`wengue`), dark charcoal (`gris-oscuro`), graphite anthracite (`antracita`), rich walnut (`nogal`), golden oak (`roble`), light gray wood (`gris-claro`), pale natural unfinished wood (`natural`) and clean satin white wood (`blanco`). Grain direction remains vertical and the two-by-two groove geometry remains unchanged.

The final nine PNGs came from nine independent built-in ImageGen calls. Twelve earlier direct results remain in `_rejected/`: ten had excess padding and two contacted one or more canvas edges closely enough that the full silhouette could not be certified. Each replacement was generated again from `original.webp`; no generated image was ever used as an input, and no accepted PNG was altered after generation.
