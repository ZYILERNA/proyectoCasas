# MADERAACÚSTICA/wl-d001 — direct ImageGen prompts

Each attempt was an independent built-in ImageGen call with `public/images/PUERTAS/MADERAACÚSTICA/wl-d001/original.webp` as its only reference.

The prompts requested one canonical finish and required the exact tall/narrow flat leaf, beveled outer frame with double inner trim, subtle vertical grain, original bottom white remate, and exactly one lever plus one round cylinder. They prohibited logos, badges, text, pseudotext, added panels, extra hardware, scenes, filters, programmatic recolor, masks, crop, resize, compositing, and generated-output references.

Rejected attempts are preserved in `_rejected/` and are not members of the active set.
