# MADERAACÚSTICA/wl-d032 — direct ImageGen prompts

Every attempt was an independent built-in ImageGen call. The only referenced image was `public/images/PUERTAS/MADERAACÚSTICA/wl-d032/original.webp`.

The prompt changed only the requested finish (`negro`, `wengue`, `gris-oscuro`, `antracita`, `nogal`, `roble`, `gris-claro`, `natural`, or `blanco`) and required:

- the exact tall/narrow front-facing door proportions;
- the complete frame, threshold, top lintel, narrow left panel and broad right panel;
- the single low horizontal seam and straight fluted block only at bottom-left;
- exactly one black lever and one circular cylinder in their original positions;
- a nearly edge-to-edge product framing on pure white;
- `NO_LOGO`: no logo, badge, wordmark, letters, numbers, symbols, text or pseudotext;
- no extra hardware, duplicated handle, scene, wall, floor or halo;
- no filters, programmatic recolor, masks, crop, resize, compositing or generated-output reference.

Attempts rejected for wide framing remain in `_rejected/` and are excluded from `manifest.json` assets.
