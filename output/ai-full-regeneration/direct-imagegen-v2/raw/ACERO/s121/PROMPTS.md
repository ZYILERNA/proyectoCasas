# ACERO/s121 direct ImageGen prompt set

All ten accepted PNG files were generated through separate built-in ImageGen calls. Every call referenced only `output/ai-full-regeneration/masters/ACERO/s121/master-no-logo-ai-tight.png`; no generated output and no logo image was used as a reference.

## Shared generation specification

- Product-mockup / ecommerce catalog image of the exact ACERO s121 door.
- Preserve the full outer frame, narrow vertically fluted left panel, broad right slab, vertical seam, very long slim recessed pull with rounded ends, compact rectangular access reader and circular knob below it.
- Straight-on portrait view; outer frame complete and only 2–5 px from the canvas edge, maximum 8 px normally and 11 px only when occupancy remains above 98%.
- Pure-white background only in the tiny perimeter; neutral studio light and realistic materials.
- Zero logos, brand marks, letters, digits, words, pseudo-text, microtext, labels, plaques, badges, captions or watermarks. The reader may retain only simple unlettered circular sensor dots.
- No invented or altered hardware, extra locks, hinges, handles, grooves or decoration.

## Finish-specific requests

- `original`: original dark charcoal-gray subtle vertical woodgrain.
- `negro`: deep near-black matte finish with subtle visible vertical woodgrain.
- `antracita`: rich graphite anthracite, darker than medium gray but distinct from pure black.
- `gris-oscuro`: sophisticated neutral dark gray, clearly lighter than anthracite black.
- `gris-claro`: elegant light neutral gray, clearly gray rather than white.
- `blanco`: both face sections in refined warm white / soft off-white with delicate vertical texture.
- `natural`: pale warm beige-tan natural timber, low saturation, not orange or yellow.
- `roble`: medium-light European oak, restrained honey-beige brown with fine vertical grain.
- `nogal`: medium-dark warm chocolate walnut with subtle straight vertical grain.
- `wengue`: very dark cool espresso brown, nearly black, with visible dark-brown vertical grain.

The accepted `original.png` is attempt 2. Attempt 1 was rejected for excess white margin. All other finishes passed on attempt 1. No filters, recoloring, masks, crop, resize, composite or format conversion were applied after generation; files were copied byte-for-byte into this staging folder.
