## Shared prompt specification

- Use case: product mockup for a tall vertical ecommerce catalog image.
- Preserve: complete black beveled frame and bottom threshold, fine vertical-ribbed leaf, narrow top panel with one horizontal seam, upper-left silver element, and slim left-side black smart lock.
- Lock: only functional geometric camera/sensor/control elements; all controls unlabelled and the remaining face smooth and blank.
- Framing: straight-on, full frame visible, 0–8 px near-white margin, more than 98% product coverage.
- Exclude: WONLY, logos, wordmarks, badges, letters, numbers, words, microtext, pseudotext, QR codes, decorative pictograms, watermarks, rooms, walls, floor scenes, props, shadows, added panels, and extra hardware.
