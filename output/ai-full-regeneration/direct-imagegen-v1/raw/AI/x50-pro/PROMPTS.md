# AI/x50-pro direct ImageGen prompts

All ten active PNGs are independent built-in ImageGen generations. Every call used only the canonical tight master as the door reference and the official WONLY logo as the branding reference. No generated finish was used as an input.

Shared prompt constraints:

- Generate a new frontal catalog render of the same complete AI X50 Pro door in the requested finish.
- Preserve the header, three vertical fields, fine ribbing, seams, full frame and threshold.
- Preserve one upper black-glass lock plate and one lower hardware module. The upper plate must be completely featureless: no letters, digits, icons, marks or microtext.
- Render exactly one official `WONLY` wordmark in the canonical upper-right position and no other text or badge.
- Fill the canvas almost edge-to-edge while keeping the full frame visible; target 0-8 px of exterior white.
- No room, wall, floor, scenery, watermark, extra hardware, duplicated hardware or rectangular patch.
- No filters, recoloring, masks, compositing, cropping or resizing after generation.

Finish requests: `original`, `negro`, `wengue`, `gris-oscuro`, `antracita`, `nogal`, `roble`, `gris-claro`, `natural`, `blanco`.

The final `natural` replacement came from built-in output `exec-a18ba470-e8f6-4626-95bd-9aee1c6ac887.png`; it was copied byte-for-byte into the active set.
